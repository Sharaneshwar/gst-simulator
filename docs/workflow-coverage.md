This reviews the app’s offered workflows against official GST tutorials and downloaded manuals. It records the completed practice scope, not certification of every GST process or complete visual/legal parity. No government filing, payment or identity verification takes place.

## Excel contract and reference workbook

- Authenticated `/api/workspace/excel/preview` is read-only. `/excel/import` reparses the same file and binds its fingerprint to the target, period and corrections. Invalid rows prevent the import; filed/offset/IFF guards apply.
- XLSX limits: 6 MB, 20 sheets, 100 columns and 2,000 invoice rows. Supported ledger exports may contain up to 20,000 source rows before voucher grouping. ZIP expansion is bounded; macros, embedded files and external links are rejected. Formula results must be cached; no spreadsheet formula runs on the server.
- Mixed-rate lines consolidate into one invoice while retaining items, original tax values and source references. Ambiguous duplicate lines, unlabelled mixed-rate tax allocations and unsupported layouts produce errors rather than guessed invoices. Non-GST payment/journal vouchers are explicitly listed as skipped.
- Register/sheet classification takes precedence over the page opened: purchase data never silently becomes outward GSTR-1 sales. Round-off/freight/discount or missing-party assumptions require review.
- The provided `Downloads/DayBook.xlsx` was read without modification. Its five purchase invoices were previewed, imported into an isolated local database and found on a subsequent GET. Totals reconciled; a rate-heading mismatch was flagged while preserving source tax amounts. No private workbook is included in Git.

## Deliberate differences and limits

- Jurisdiction defaults are training data. Exact real ward/range mapping needs the instructor’s verified import. Postal locations are a reference snapshot; map coordinates are approximate and do not reverse-geocode street addresses. See [postal-data-sources.md](postal-data-sources.md).
- Identity uploads, PAN/Aadhaar responses, signing, banks, IRP and EWB services are simulated. There are no live government, bank or transporter validations. The generated QR is not a government digital signature.
- The calculator implements standard rates/caps and explicit exercise overrides, not automatic ingestion of every historical statutory extension or relief. The legacy pre-2021 reporting window is simplified; modern 30-November and annual-return cutoffs are covered.
- Supported Excel formats are a convenience for this app, not a claim that GSTN directly accepts Tally workbooks or that every offline utility worksheet/export is supported.
- Separate GST return types, tax-officer clarification/rejection/cancellation back offices and every NIC edge case remain outside the currently offered practice scope. This completion does not claim all downloaded manuals are implemented.

## Verification

- Backend: 164 tests, including isolated HTTP workflows for PMT-09 OTP/replay/balance binding, queued correction, ECO originals, IMS snapshots, negative values, master import and postal lookup.
- Frontend: 32 tests covering entry calculations, address behaviour and rendered workflow controls.
- `npm run build` passes. MapLibre is lazy-loaded; Vite reports its map chunk size as a warning.
- Prior local browser checks covered invoice save/reopen, full-page ITC/payment, mobile rate-grid layout, street maps/confirmation, and sample proof upload success without a picker.
- Reference-workbook parsing and persistence were verified through the local HTTP API. Browser automation’s real-file chooser did not pass; no claim is made otherwise.
- Final browser reattachment failed with a browser-control availability error. The newest screens have component-render and HTTP checks, but no fresh interactive browser sign-off in this batch.
- All automated databases and email deliveries were temporary local fixtures/mocks. Production student work was not reset or migrated.
